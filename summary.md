1. **One company absorbed the day.** At least 16 of the 1,000 tracked HN stories referenced Nvidia or Huang — the most-commented thread (1,264 comments), a France24 TV debate, a Washington policy story, an astroturfing report, a smuggling report. When one name is simultaneously bellwether, export-control party, and governance scandal, a single stock becomes a macro instrument — and the timestamps show the scandal escalating publicly within two hours.
